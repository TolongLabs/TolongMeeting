# TolongMeeting design (split)

This page used to hold the whole design of TolongMeeting, revision 2 (20 October 2026). On 20 October 2026 it was split into four documents, each with one concern: [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) says what the product is, the [PRD](../PRD.md) says what it must do, the [TRD](../TRD.md) says how it is built, and the [design](../DESIGN.md) says what the member sees. Nothing was removed; the table below shows where each old section went. Old links into this page, and the old anchors, no longer resolve here: use the new homes.
