# Research

Research gathered on 20 October 2026 while designing TolongMeeting. It covers existing open-source meeting bots, what the meeting platforms allow, the AI providers members can bring their own keys for, and three specialist reviews of the design. Everything here is dated; re-check before relying on it.

Contents:

1.  [Documents](#documents)
1.  [How it was gathered](#how-it-was-gathered)

## Documents

- [Open-source meeting bots](open-source-meeting-bots.md): Attendee, Vexa, joinly, screenappai/meeting-bot and others, compared against the app's needs, and why the design builds its own browser bot.
- [Platform constraints](platform-constraints.md): Zoom, Teams and Google Meet rules for automated participants, form sign-in requirements and QR decoders.
- [AI providers](ai-providers.md): DashScope and ModelScope endpoints, models, prices, speech recognition and data use.
- [Expert reviews](expert-reviews/README.md): three specialist reviews of the design and the triage of every finding.

## How it was gathered

- **The first three documents**: parallel research agents (Claude Haiku 5.5 at high effort) using web search and official documentation. Their findings were summarised during the brainstorm and written up here with sources.
- **Expert reviews**: Gemini 4 Argon (high) through the Antigravity CLI.
- Facts marked as forum, vendor or secondary sources are unverified.
